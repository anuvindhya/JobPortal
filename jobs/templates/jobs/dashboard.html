Welcome to JobPortal

A platform connecting talented job seekers with employers.

Find opportunities, post vacancies, and manage job applications in one place.

Project Overview

JobPortal is a web-based job management system developed using Python, Django, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and SQLite. It allows employers to publish job vacancies and job seekers to search and apply for suitable jobs.

Roles and Responsibilities

Employer

Responsibilities:

  • Register and log in as an employer.
  • Create and publish job vacancies.
  • Update job descriptions and details.
  • Delete job postings.
  • View and manage posted jobs.

Job Seeker

Responsibilities:

  • Register and log in as a job seeker.
  • Browse available job vacancies.
  • View job descriptions and salary details.
  • Apply for suitable job opportunities.
  • View submitted applications.

Technologies Used

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, Bootstrap
  • Backend: Python, Django
  • Database: SQLite
  • Authentication: Django Authentication
